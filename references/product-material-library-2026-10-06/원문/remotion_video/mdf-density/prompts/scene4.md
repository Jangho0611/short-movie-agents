# Scene4 Prompt

Use together with common.md (Character Lock, Workshop Lock, Camera Style, Lighting, Video Spec, Negative Prompt).

## Purpose

Show a real-world consequence — a bookshelf shelf sagged from long-term use with an unsuitable low-density MDF. Convey empathy through an already-aged, well-used shelf, not a time-passing transition.

## Subject

The same sagging bookshelf from Scene1, worn and dusty, with faded book spines resting on the sagging shelf board.

## Environment

Same workshop area, near the bookshelf seen earlier, now shown up close.

## Action

The craftsman crouches slightly beside the bookshelf and runs two fingers slowly along the underside of the sagging shelf board, following its downward curve. The shelf's aged, worn condition is already present in frame — no transition, no time-lapse, no before/after cut.

## Camera

Handheld close-up following the hand as it traces the sagging shelf board. Single continuous shot. No cuts.

## Important Details

Wear and aging must already be present in the shot — do not depict the passage of time itself.

The entire scene should feel like a real documentary filmed inside an actual Korean woodworking shop. Nothing should look staged.
