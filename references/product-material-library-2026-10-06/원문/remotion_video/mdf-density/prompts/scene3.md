## Purpose (체감 비교 — felt-difference comparison, not a lab test)

Show that two MDF boards of the same size and thickness can feel different in stiffness when handled — without presenting it as a formal test or claiming density alone determines all performance.

## Planned content

- Two MDF boards, same size and same thickness.
- No face. Hands and arms only.
- Each board's one end is held and gently lifted/flexed to show a felt difference in stiffness — a single, simple action per board.
- No excessive bending, no forcing deformation, no breaking.
- Compare one product at a time, or a same-condition side-by-side split if that composition proves feasible — not decided yet.
- Do not stage this like formal test equipment (no calipers, no lab bench, no measuring rig).
- Product name, grade ("15형"/"35형" etc.), and any numbers are NOT generated in Veo — added in Remotion post-production only.

## Messaging guardrails (see common.md / video-workflow.md "사실 표현 주의")

- Do not claim density alone determines all warping/performance.
- Do not present the hand-flex comparison as a certified or scientific test.
- Approved phrasing direction only, e.g. "제품에 따라 단단하게 느껴지는 정도가 다를 수 있습니다" — never absolute claims like "밀도가 높으면 절대 휘지 않습니다."

## Narration (added in post-production, not in the Veo clip)

"같은 규격이라도 제품에 따라 단단하게 느껴지는 정도가 다를 수 있습니다."
