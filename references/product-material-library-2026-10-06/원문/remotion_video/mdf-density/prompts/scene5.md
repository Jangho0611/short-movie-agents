# Scene5 Prompt

Use together with common.md (Character Lock, Workshop Lock, Camera Style, Lighting, Video Spec, Negative Prompt).

## Purpose

Close the documentary with a calm and trustworthy workshop scene focused on the craftsman returning one hand tool to its wall rack, as the final live-action shot immediately before the fixed brand ending sequence.

## Subject

A single hand tool being returned to its place.

## Environment

Same workshop, tidy state, wall tool rack.

## Action

Return one hand tool to the wall rack calmly.

## Camera

Slow handheld shot following the craftsman's hand as he places the tool on the wall rack. Single continuous shot. No cuts.

## Important Details

Calm, quiet, trustworthy mood suitable as the lead-in to a brand closing sequence.

The entire scene should feel like a real documentary filmed inside an actual Korean woodworking shop. Nothing should look staged.
